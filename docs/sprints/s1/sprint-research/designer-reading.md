# A designer's reading of the front page

A web designer read the framework's front page on 23 November 2026, the day
it was redrawn as one picture. The owner passed their words on as two
pictures of a conversation. The designer is not named here; they did not
write for this record.

## What was said

Their messages were private, so they are told here and not copied out; only
a few of their phrases are kept.

Of the page as a whole they said they saw what it was going for but wanted
more of something they found hard to name: "some more play", "some more
world, some more place", and one word more, "rex". They pointed the owner
to a video game, Noita, as work in a kindred style to take inspiration
from. And they said they were loving the letterforms.

The owner then put it to them as hors d'oeuvre served where an entree was
needed. They agreed, and said the type and the colours were strong, but
that for a kit it had "great walls", a carpet they loved, and no furniture.

## How it is read

- **The letters and the colours are right.** Said three ways ("Loving the
  letterforms", "Strong typo, strong colours", "great walls love the
  carpet"). Nothing in this asks for Cruor or the palette to change.
- **The furniture is missing.** This is the plain criticism. A kit is for
  building with, and the framework offers fonts and a front page; there is
  no button, field, table, card or bar of navigation to take.
- **"Play", "world", "place"** are read as the same lack from the other
  side. A place is somewhere things can be done. The front page is a
  picture: it can be looked at and not touched.
- **The game named** is one whose world is made of matter that behaves:
  every grain falls, burns, pools and runs. It is taken as a pointer to
  that quality (things that respond, a world with its own rules) and not as
  a look to copy.
- **"rex"** is not understood. It may be a slip of the keys. The owner may
  ask.

## What it changed

The owner chose on 24 November 2026 to make the next sprint the look with
its furniture and a showroom ("Start that sprint"). Two intents record it:
[INT-0006](../../../intents/INT-0006-the-furniture.md) and
[INT-0007](../../../intents/INT-0007-the-showroom.md).

A sketch made between the designer's two messages (stone at the page's
sides, candles, the statue's blood running down the page, moving) answered
the first message literally. It is not in the repository. It added to what
was already said to be right, and is set aside.
