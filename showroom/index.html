The narthex

Cruor is the Latin for blood that has been shed, and this is a place lettered in it. Walk down it. Press what can be pressed, write in what can be written in, open what is shut. Nothing here needs a script to work, and beside each thing is the HTML that makes it.

What is cut in obsidian is not lightly unsaid.

A page takes this look on by linking four stylesheets and setting one class on its body. The scriptorium, at the foot of this place, shows how.

The scriptorium

The letters of this place are Cruor's. The title of a page is cut in Cruor Thornamental Regular, the headings beneath it in Cruor Lite Regular, and what is read at length in Cruor Sans Regular. Each is one line of the theme file, theme.toml, and is changed there.

Taking the look on

A page links the kit's four stylesheets and sets one class on its body. Plain HTML is then dressed with no further markup, and a part is asked for by its class.
